    Practice • Nouns

    🔎 Noun Detective ✨

    Let's explore the magical world and discover nouns!

    📚 What are Nouns?

    Nouns are naming words. They name people, places, animals and things.

    💡 Two Kinds of Names

    Common Noun — a general name, like dragon.
    Proper Noun — a special name, like Flame. It starts with a capital letter!

    ⭐ Today's Mission

    Explore the world, find Common Nouns, and give some of them special names to make Proper Nouns!

    MISSION 1 · DISCOVER

    🔎 Look Carefully!

    Can you find something we can name? Click a glowing clue in the magical world!

    Let's investigate! Click on something glowing in the magical world.

    This is a noun — a naming word!

    Great find! A noun names something. Let's figure out what kind of noun this is.
    MISSION 2 · CLASSIFY

    📋 Classify the Noun

    Is it a Common Noun or a Proper Noun?

    Think carefully! Is this a general name, or a special name for one particular thing?
